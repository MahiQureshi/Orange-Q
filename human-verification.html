! Prototype and validation notice

ORANGE-Q AI is currently a prototype demonstration. The screening classifications, confidence values and quality scores shown in this report are not laboratory diagnoses and should not be interpreted as scientifically validated disease detection. Visual screening cannot by itself establish genetic true-to-type status. True-to-type verification may require additional sensing, reference material, molecular testing or other validated methods. Uncertain cases should be escalated to trained human verification. Final deployment should be validated using locally relevant nursery datasets, multiple lighting conditions, independent field validation and measured outcomes such as post-transplant survival.
